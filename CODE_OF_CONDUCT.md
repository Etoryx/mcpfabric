# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The MCP Fabric community is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive
environment. Participate with empathy, assume good intent, and critique ideas and code rather than
people.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving actionable, evidence-based feedback;
- respecting different experience levels, identities, and viewpoints;
- keeping issues and pull requests focused on the project;
- protecting other people's privacy and never posting credentials or bridge tokens.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and
publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, release discussions, and
other spaces where someone represents the MCP Fabric project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation, contact the repository maintainers. For a sensitive or private report, use
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Etoryx/mcpfabric/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report with `Conduct report`. Do not open a public issue containing private details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behavior threatens the safety or productivity of the community. Reports will be reviewed as
privately and promptly as practical, with conflicts of interest avoided.
